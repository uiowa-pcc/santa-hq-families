# Santa HQ planning flow

Replace index.html, santa-hq-details.css, and sw.js at the root of your Santa HQ GitHub repository. Commit those files to trigger the usual Cloudflare deployment. This package includes the recent season redesign and Gift Board updates. It requires no Worker or database changes.

- Home has one Gift Board entry point and a list of Christmas activities. The next dated tradition appears beside Christmas Plans when available.
- Plan Gifts puts Gift Board, Christmas Morning, and Shopping List within reach. Add a quick idea directly to the current recipient, or use More options for a link, photo, or pasted list.
- Drag gifts to arrange them. Confirmed gifts sort to the top of their pile and appear in Christmas Morning. Confirmed gifts you still need to buy also appear in Shopping.
- Christmas Plans opens into the family checklists, with Traditions, Shopping, and Lights Hunt in its navigation.
