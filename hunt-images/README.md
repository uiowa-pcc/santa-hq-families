# Add your own example hunt photos

Upload photos here from GitHub: `hunt-images/`. Supported types: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP. Cloudflare's `prepare-assets.mjs` creates a photo manifest automatically on deployment.

The filename matches the clue name in lowercase, with punctuation/spaces replaced by hyphens. Examples:
- `mickey-mouse.jpg`
- `winnie-the-pooh.jpg`
- `the-grinch.jpg`
- `bluey.jpg`
- `stitch.jpg`
- `hello-kitty.jpg`
- `rudolph.png`
- `jack-skellington.webp`
- `snoopy.jpg`
- `olaf.jpg`
- `lights-spelling-a-word.jpg`

These are **example clue art** for the hunt—not player-submitted photos. In Photo Hunt mode, players' photos are uploaded separately to private Cloudflare R2 storage and replace the examples on their own boards. Shared submissions appear in the in-app family feed, never in GitHub.

Use photos you own or have permission to reuse. Avoid GPS tags, house numbers, and children’s faces.
